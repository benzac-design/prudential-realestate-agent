5 Core Use Cases
Use Case 01

Instant Lead Follow-Up

The moment a lead fills out a contact form, the AI sends a personalised SMS and email within seconds — before any competitor can respond.

Trigger New lead form submitted → SMS + Email sent automatically
Use Case 02

Automatic Showing Bookings

The AI qualifies leads by asking about budget, timeline, and pre-approval status, then books a showing or call via Calendly — no back-and-forth needed.

Trigger Lead replies with interest → Booking link + qualification questions sent
Use Case 03

AI Listing Descriptions

Agent enters property details and the AI writes a professional 150-200 word MLS listing, then automatically audits it for Fair Housing Act compliance.

Trigger Agent submits property details → Listing + compliance audit returned instantly
Use Case 04

60-Day Follow-Up Sequence

Every lead automatically receives 5 follow-up messages at Day 3, 7, 14, 30, and 60 — each AI-written and personalised to keep the relationship warm.

Trigger Lead saved → Sequence scheduled, runs automatically every 30 mins
Use Case 05

Weekly Client Update Emails

Every buyer client receives a weekly AI-written email with new matching listings, market updates, and an offer to schedule viewings — keeping them engaged.

Trigger Agent clicks "Send Updates" → All buyer clients emailed in one click
Bonus Feature

Fair Housing Compliance Audit

Every generated listing is automatically scanned for Fair Housing Act violations — flagging risky phrases, listing violations, and suggesting exact fixes.

Result PASS ✅ or FAIL ❌ with violation list and suggested replacement text
